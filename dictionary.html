English Dictionary

1. Apple

Definition: A sweet fruit with red or green skin and a crisp inside.

Example: She brought an apple for lunch.

2. Book

Definition: A collection of printed or written pages bound together.

Example: He read a science fiction book last night.

3. Computer

Definition: An electronic device for storing and processing data.

Example: I use my computer to complete school projects.

4. Dog

Definition: A domesticated animal known for loyalty and friendliness.

Example: The dog barked loudly at the stranger.

5. Elephant

Definition: A large mammal with a trunk, found mostly in Africa and Asia.

Example: The elephant sprayed water with its trunk.

6. Flower

Definition: The colorful part of a plant that often smells good.

Example: She picked a flower from the garden.

7. Guitar

Definition: A musical instrument with strings played by strumming or plucking.

Example: He played the guitar at the concert.

8. House

Definition: A building for people to live in.

Example: Their house has a red roof and white walls.

9. Ice Cream

Definition: A cold sweet dessert made from milk or cream.

Example: She loves eating chocolate ice cream in summer.

10. Jungle

Definition: A dense forest in a tropical area.

Example: The explorer got lost in the jungle.
